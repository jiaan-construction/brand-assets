## Colours

- Brand red: `#E9272C`
- Primary black: `#111111`
- Secondary grey: `#888888`
- Light background: `#E9EEF5`

## Basic rules

1. Preserve the original aspect ratio.
2. Leave clear space around the logo.
3. Do not change colours, redraw, crop, stretch, or add effects.
4. Use the complete legal name: **JIA AN CONSTRUCTION PTE. LTD.**
5. Use the supplied letterhead as-is unless the company issues a newer version.
